Refund Policy

Clear, fair, and transparent refund guidelines for our clients

Our Approach

At Sharda Publication, we are dedicated to delivering high-quality academic and publication services tailored to your needs. We value trust, transparency, and long-term relationships with our clients.

While we strive for complete satisfaction, we understand that certain situations may require refund consideration. Our policies are designed to balance fairness with the effort invested in each project.

Refund Guidelines

Case-by-Case Evaluation

Every refund request is reviewed individually based on project status and specific circumstances.

Before Work Begins

If cancellation is requested before any work starts, a full or partial refund may be considered.

After Work Has Started

Once work such as writing, editing, or submission support has begun, refunds are generally not applicable.

7-Day Reporting Window

Any concerns regarding quality or delivery must be reported within 7 days of receiving the deliverables.

Journal Decisions

Acceptance or rejection depends on journals. Therefore, rejection alone does not qualify for a refund.

Important Note

Publication outcomes are controlled by journals and external reviewers. While we guide and support you throughout the process, we cannot guarantee acceptance. Our focus is on improving quality and increasing your chances of successful publication.

Our Commitment

We aim to handle every refund request professionally, ensuring fairness and transparency. Your satisfaction and trust remain our top priorities.
